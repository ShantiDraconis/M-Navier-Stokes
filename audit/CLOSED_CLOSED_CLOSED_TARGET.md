## Important role of I

The independent I-core is genuinely CLOSED. However, it does not automatically
belong to the Navier-Stokes critical path. It may be used in the final chain
only if a proved theorem consumes its definitions/certificates and produces a
later NS-stage result.

Therefore:
- I_core_closed is valid;
- I -> NS is not created by naming it;
- if no theorem uses I in M0..M6, I remains a certified auxiliary theory;
- if an I-based bridge is proved, that bridge itself must be CLOSED and audited.

## Current mathematical reality

The present corpus has already kernel-checked substantial infrastructure and
the algebraic implication from flexible stretching control to a
Gronwall-ready differential inequality.

It does not yet contain concrete theorem proofs for every M0..M6 edge.
Consequently the strict all-closed prize certificate must remain absent/RED.
This is intentional: the target workflow must fail rather than relabel an open
Navier-Stokes theorem as closed.
